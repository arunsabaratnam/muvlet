# Muvlet

Muvlet is a mobile fitness app designed to help beginners become confident and capable in the gym. It brings workout planning, exercise instruction, progress tracking, nutrition education and recovery guidance into a simple, modern interface.

At the centre of Muvlet is a team of small, animated AI characters. Each character specializes in a different part of the fitness experience, helping users understand what to do, how to do it and how to build a routine that works for them. As users gain experience, they can customize their workouts and choose how much guidance they receive.
